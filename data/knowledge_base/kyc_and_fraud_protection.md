# NidhiVani AI - KYC Guidelines, Fraud Prevention, and Security Policies

## 1. Know Your Customer (KYC) Verification & Periodic Updation (Re-KYC)
* **Officially Valid Documents (OVD)**: In accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions, only the following documents are accepted for KYC identity and address verification:
  1. Passport
  2. Driving Licence
  3. Proof of possession of Aadhaar number (masked Aadhaar showing only the last 4 digits)
  4. Voter's Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India
  5. Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
  6. Letter issued by the National Population Register (NPR) containing details of name and address
* **Permanent Account Number (PAN)**: Mandatory for opening savings accounts and conducting financial transactions exceeding ₹50,000. In the absence of PAN, Form 60 must be submitted.
* **Re-KYC Intervals**:
  * High-Risk Customers: Once every 2 years.
  * Medium-Risk Customers: Once every 8 years.
  * Low-Risk Customers: Once every 10 years.
  * Re-KYC can be completed fully online via Video-KYC (V-CIP) or at any physical branch.

## 2. Cyber Fraud Reporting, Unauthorized Transactions & Customer Liability
* **National Cyber Crime Helpline Number**: Dial **1930** immediately to report unauthorized digital transactions or cyber fraud.
* **National Cyber Crime Portal**: Official government reporting at `https://cybercrime.gov.in`.
* **Bank Incident Reporting**: Contact NidhiVani Fraud Management Desk at `fraud@nidhivani.in` or call toll-free `1800-123-NIDHI`.
* **Zero Customer Liability Window (RBI Mandate)**:
  * If an unauthorized electronic banking transaction occurs due to contributory fraud, negligence, or deficiency on the part of the bank, customer liability is **Zero**.
  * If unauthorized transaction occurs due to third-party breach where neither the bank nor the customer is at fault, customer liability is **Zero** if reported within **3 working days (72 hours)** of receiving alert.
  * If reported within 4 to 7 working days, customer liability is capped up to ₹10,000 for standard savings accounts. Beyond 7 days, liability is determined by bank policy board approval.

## 3. Cards & Digital Channel Clarifications
* **Debit & Credit Cards**: NidhiVani AI operates as an ultra-modern, digital-first conversational branch. NidhiVani AI **does not issue physical plastic debit or credit cards**. All transactions are authorized via biometrics, UPI rails, or secure online transfers.
* **Phishing & Credential Safety**: Bank representatives will **NEVER** ask for your MPIN, one-time passwords (OTP), login passwords, or biometric passphrase. Never share your 4-digit MPIN with anyone.
