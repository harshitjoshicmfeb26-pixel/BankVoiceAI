# NidhiVani AI - Interest Rates and Service Charges Policy

## 1. Savings Account Interest Rates
* **Balances up to ₹1,00,000**: 3.00% per annum.
* **Balances above ₹1,00,000 up to ₹10,00,000**: 3.50% per annum.
* **Balances above ₹10,00,000**: 4.00% per annum.
* **Calculation and Payout**: Interest is calculated on daily end-of-day balances and credited quarterly to customer accounts on June 30, September 30, December 31, and March 31.

## 2. Fixed Deposit (FD) Slabs & Tenures
* **7 Days to 45 Days**: 3.50% per annum.
* **46 Days to 179 Days**: 4.75% per annum.
* **180 Days to 364 Days**: 5.50% per annum.
* **1 Year (365 Days)**: 6.80% per annum.
* **Over 1 Year up to 3 Years**: 7.10% per annum (Highest yield standard slab).
* **Above 3 Years up to 5 Years**: 6.75% per annum.
* **5 Years Tax-Saving FD**: 6.50% per annum (Locked-in period of 5 years under Section 80C).
* **Senior Citizen Privilege**: Additional 0.50% (50 basis points) preferential interest across all tenures for Indian resident citizens aged 60 and above. For example, 1-year FD for senior citizens yields 7.30% per annum.
* **Premature Withdrawal Penalty**: 0.50% deduction from applicable interest rate for the period the deposit remained with the bank. No penalty if withdrawn after 2 years on 3-year deposits.

## 3. Recurring Deposit (RD) Rates
* **Minimum Monthly Deposit**: ₹500 (in multiples of ₹100).
* **Tenure Range**: 6 months to 10 years.
* **Interest Rate**: Equivalent to the Fixed Deposit rate for the corresponding tenure.
* **Senior Citizen Benefit**: Additional 0.50% per annum applicable.

## 4. Retail Lending and Loan Interest Rates
* **Home Loans**: Starting at 8.40% per annum (Floating rate linked to RBI External Benchmark Lending Rate / EBLR).
* **Car / Auto Loans**: Fixed at 8.75% per annum for new vehicles (tenures up to 7 years).
* **Personal Loans**: Starting at 10.50% to 13.50% per annum based on credit score (minimum CIBIL score 720 required).
* **Education Loans**: 8.85% per annum with 0.50% concession for female students.
* **Gold Loans**: 8.25% per annum for agricultural and personal needs with instant valuation.

## 5. Service Charges, Penalties and Transfer Fees
* **Digital Funds Transfers (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS)**: Free (₹0) on all digital, mobile, and voice banking channels in accordance with RBI digital guidelines.
* **Minimum Balance Non-Maintenance Charge**: ₹150 per quarter if average quarterly balance drops below branch threshold.
* **Cheque Return / Bounce Penalty (Financial reasons)**: ₹350 per instance.
* **Account Statement Generation (Email / PDF)**: Free (₹0) on voice request and web portal.
* **Physical Duplicate Statement (at Branch)**: ₹50 per statement.
