# NidhiVani AI - Banking Operations, Transaction Limits, and Account Policies

## 1. Domestic Fund Transfer Limits & Operating Timings
* **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)**:
  * Operating Window: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year (including bank holidays).
  * Per-Transaction Limit: Minimum ₹1, Maximum ₹2,00,000 per transaction.
  * Daily Cumulative Limit: ₹5,00,000 per account per day.
  * Settlement: Real-time instantaneous credit to recipient.
* **NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)**:
  * Operating Window: 24x7 automated batch settlement every 30 minutes.
  * Transaction Limits: No minimum or maximum regulatory limit on digital channels.
  * Daily Digital Limit: Up to ₹10,00,000 per day for pre-registered beneficiaries.
* **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)**:
  * Operating Window: 24x7 continuous real-time gross settlement.
  * Transaction Limit: Minimum transaction amount is ₹2,00,000. No upper ceiling limit.
  * Designed for large-value institutional and high-value personal transfers.

## 2. Voice Banking Transfer Security Cap (Fraud Mitigation Rule)
* **Voice-Initiated Transfer Limit**: Maximum **₹10,000 per transaction** and maximum **₹25,000 daily limit** through conversational voice channels.
* **Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Every voice fund transfer requires explicit 4-digit MPIN validation and customer confirmation before execution.
* **Higher Value Transfers**: Transfers exceeding ₹10,000 must be executed via the authenticated Internet Banking dashboard or in-branch verification to safeguard against acoustic spoofing and duress.

## 3. Minimum Average Balance (MAB) Requirements
* **Metro and Urban Branches**: Minimum Average Quarterly Balance (AQB) of ₹3,000.
* **Semi-Urban Branches**: Minimum Average Quarterly Balance of ₹1,500.
* **Rural Branches**: Minimum Average Quarterly Balance of ₹1,000.
* **Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA / Jan Dhan)**: Zero balance account (no minimum balance required, zero non-maintenance penalty).

## 4. Account Statements and Transaction Records
* **Digital Statements**: Customers can request account statements for the last 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or custom date ranges via voice commands or online banking. Generated PDF statements are sent instantly to registered email addresses with zero fees.
* **Passbook Updates**: Digital e-passbook is available in real time on the portal. Physical passbook printing is available at all automated branch kiosks.
