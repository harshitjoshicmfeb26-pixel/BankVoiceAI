## 2. Working Hours and Holiday Schedules
* **Operating Hours**: Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM for public transactions (Branch remains open till 5:00 PM for non-cash administrative requests).
* **Saturday Schedule**:
  * **Open**: 1st, 3rd, and 5th Saturday of every month (10:00 AM to 2:00 PM).
  * **Closed**: 2nd and 4th Saturday of every month (Mandated national bank holiday).
* **Sunday & National Holidays**: All physical branches remain closed. Digital voice banking and web portals operate 24x7x365.
